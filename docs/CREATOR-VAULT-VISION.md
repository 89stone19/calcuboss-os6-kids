## 🇿🇦 Executive Summary & Core Concept

**Problem:** Millions of South African youth face post-matric education dropouts due to NSFAS bursary failures or high university costs, despite having natural practical skills.

**Solution:** The **OS6 Kids Creator Trust™** empowers Grade 1 to 12 learners to create and record practical skill educational videos (boilermaking math, electrical fundamentals, agricultural science, coding, and measurement math). 

- **Channel Umbrella:** Videos are uploaded via the official **Calcuboss OS6** umbrella on YouTube Kids under the official `@DerolWillis` channel.
- **75/25 Vault Revenue Split:**
  - **75%** is locked directly into the learner's **OS6 Educational Trust Vault**, accumulating compound growth and releasing when the child reaches age 18 for tertiary tuition or vocational tooling.
  - **25%** goes to platform maintenance, server operations, and community voucher pools.

## 🛡️ Legal, Regulatory & Compliance Framework

1. **YouTube Kids Compliance (COPPA & Under-13 Rules):**
   - Under-13 creators cannot upload directly or operate independent YouTube channels.
   - The Calcuboss OS6 platform acts as the verified publishing publisher umbrella using `@DerolWillis` verified channel keys.
2. **AI Safety Scanner (`isKidsVideo`):**
   - Every submitted clip is automatically pre-screened by Ms Nova & Calcuboss AI for kids educational keywords (`math`, `science`, `phonics`, `boilermaking`, `practical`) and scanned against safety filters before parent sign-off.
3. **Guardian / Parent Consent:**
   - Requires verified parent ID sign-off and guardian consent agreement prior to video submission and vault opening.
4. **Financial Trust Structure:**
   - Funds are managed via a Section 18A Educational Trust account / Paystack Split Vault hold. Individual payouts are restricted until proof of age 18 or accredited tertiary enrollment.
